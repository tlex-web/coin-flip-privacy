Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

3D Coin Flip is a native Android app with a single purpose: flipping a coin to help you make a decision. This policy describes what the app does, and does not, do with your data.

No internet access, no accounts, no ads, no trackers, no analytics. 3D Coin Flip requests zero Android permissions, and nothing you do here leaves your phone.

The app requests no Android permissions of any kind. It has no network access, so it cannot send or receive anything over the internet. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. Nothing is collected, stored remotely, shared with any third party, or sold. Because nothing is collected, there is no data to request access to, correct, or delete.

Since the app collects nothing, there is no personal data held anywhere for this policy to describe further. If a future version of the app changes this in any way, this policy will be updated accordingly and the effective date above will change.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at coinflip-privacy@tlex.dev.
